Our Founders

The people
behind the forest.

Krishna Green Foundation was built by two founders who believe that lasting environmental change begins with accountability — measured in survival, not saplings planted.

Founders & Directors
KK
Founder & Director

Krishna Kishore Kammila

Krishna Kishore Kammila is a Founder and Director of Krishna Green Foundation. He is committed to advancing the Foundation's vision of a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable future. Through his leadership, he supports initiatives that encourage environmental responsibility, community participation, and meaningful action for the planet.

His work reflects a belief that lasting environmental change begins with awareness, collaboration, and practical solutions at the community level.

KS
Founder & Director

Krishna Sahithi Sakamuri

Krishna Sahithi Sakamuri is a Founder and Director of Krishna Green Foundation. She is passionate about creating positive environmental and social impact through community-focused sustainability initiatives.

As a leader of the Foundation, she helps shape its mission to inspire people to care for the environment and contribute to a healthier future. Her commitment reflects the belief that collective action, education, and everyday choices can create lasting change.
