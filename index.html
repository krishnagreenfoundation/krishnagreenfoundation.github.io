How we work

Survival isn't luck. It's four disciplines, held from soil to canopy.

01 — Assess

Assess the land

We survey soil, water and the existing ecology of each reserve-forest compartment or park before a single pit is dug — so the plan fits the ground, not the other way around.

02 — Prepare

Prepare & select

Pitting, soil work and fencing come first, and we choose native, site-suited species [KEY_SPECIES] raised to plantable size — the two decisions that most determine whether saplings live.

03 — Plant

Plant with the monsoon

Planting is timed to the rains and executed at scale — the reason a 35-acre compartment can go from bare to 35,000 established saplings in a single season.

04 — Nurture

Nurture & verify

We water, weed, protect and re-fill gaps through the vulnerable early years, and record survival site by site. The 100% figure is an outcome we measure, not a claim we make.

About

A Section 8 non-profit built to be a serious CSR implementation partner — measured on what lives, not what's launched.

Krishna Green Foundation is a not-for-profit (Section 8) company and CSR implementation partner working across Andhra Pradesh. We take on afforestation and greening projects on behalf of funders and forest agencies, and hold ourselves to a single, verifiable standard: the saplings we plant have to survive. Across nine sites and 297 acres, they have.

How we plan, deliver and measure

Every project follows the same four disciplines — assess, prepare, plant, nurture — and every site enters a register with its area, sapling count and recorded survival. That register is the version of transparency we can stand behind: a funder can point to a specific compartment and see exactly what was planted there and how much of it lived.
